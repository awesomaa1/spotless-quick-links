## 📱 How to Generate Your QR Code

1. Once you put this page live (e.g., `https://www.spotlessexitcleaning.com.au/links`), copy that URL.
2. Go to any free high-resolution QR generator (such as [qr-code-generator.com](https://www.qr-code-generator.com/) or [canva.com](https://canva.com)).
3. Paste your link.
4. Download the QR code as **SVG** or **high-res PNG**.
5. Print it on:
   - **Business cards & Fridge Magnets** (given to tenants during end-of-lease inspections).
   - **Airbnb welcome binders & turnover checklists**.
   - **Flyers in apartment building lobbies & letterboxes**.
   - **Company vans, trailers, and uniforms**.
   - **Final invoice emails and printed receipts**.
